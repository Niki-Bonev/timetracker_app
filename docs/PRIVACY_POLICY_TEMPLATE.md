## Information you choose to store

Tempo stores projects, session times, breaks, notes, goals and related settings so the app can provide time tracking and insights. In local mode this information is stored on your device.

If you choose to sign in, Tempo uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to provide account access, backup and synchronization. Depending on the sign-in method, account data may include your email address, display name, profile image and a service user identifier. Your Tempo project and session data is stored under that account identifier.

## How information is used

The information is used to run the timer, show history and insights, synchronize data when requested, restore data across signed-in devices, and support account management. Tempo does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics in this release.

## Sharing

Tempo does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate optional sign-in and sync, including Google/Firebase, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. Exports are shared only when you initiate an export through Android's share interface.
